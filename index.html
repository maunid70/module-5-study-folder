    Equation sheet

    Constants are given on quizzes and tests. Know what each variable means and which equation links the two quantities you have.

    c = λν
    Speed of light = wavelength × frequency. λ in meters, ν in s−1 (Hz).
    E = hν = hc/λ
    Energy of one photon, in joules.
    En = −2.178 × 10−18 J (Z2/n2)
    Bohr energy level. One-electron species only (H, He+, Li2+). Z = nuclear charge.
    ΔE = Efinal − Einitial
    Negative ΔE: photon emitted (electron falls). Positive: photon absorbed.
    1/λ = R(1/nlow2 − 1/nhigh2)
    Balmer–Rydberg equation for hydrogen. R = 1.097 × 107 m−1.
    KE = Ephoton − Φ
    Photoelectric effect. Φ is the work function (minimum energy to free an electron). KE = ½mv2.
    λ = h/mv
    de Broglie wavelength of a moving particle. m in kg, v in m/s.
    Δx · Δp ≥ h/4π
    Heisenberg uncertainty principle. p = mv.
    A = εbc
    Beer–Lambert law. ε in L/(mol·cm), b = path length in cm, c in mol/L.
    A = −log T
    Absorbance from transmittance (T as a fraction). The colorimeter in the penny lab reads A directly.
    M1V1 = M2V2
    Dilution: the concentration of each penny-lab standard.
    Zeff ≈ Z − S
    Effective nuclear charge. S = number of core (shielding) electrons.
    F ∝ q1q2/r2
    Coulomb’s law. Bigger charges or smaller distance mean a stronger attraction.
    c = 2.998 × 108 m/s
    Speed of light.
    h = 6.626 × 10−34 J·s
    Planck’s constant.
    NA = 6.022 × 1023 mol−1
    Per photon × NA = per mole. Divide by 1000 for kJ/mol.
    1 nm = 10−9 m
    Visible light is about 400 nm (violet) to 700 nm (red).

    Trends at a glance

    PropertyAcross a period (→)Down a group (↓)Why
    Atomic radiusDecreasesIncreasesAcross: more protons, same core shielding, so higher Zeff pulls the shell in. Down: a new shell farther out.
    First ionization energyIncreases (dips at B and O)DecreasesHigher Zeff and smaller r mean a stronger Coulomb attraction to the electron removed.
    Electron affinityGenerally more energy released (exceptions: Be, N, noble gases)No clean trendA higher Zeff attracts the added electron more strongly.
    ElectronegativityIncreasesDecreasesSame reasoning as ionization energy, for electrons shared in a bond. F is highest.
    Metallic characterDecreasesIncreasesLow ionization energy means electrons are given up or shared easily.
    Ion sizeCation smaller than its atom; anion larger. Isoelectronic ions: more protons, smaller ion.Fewer electrons per proton (or a lost shell) pulls the cloud in.
